# Output formats

## Match the length to the ask

- **A question gets a short answer.** One screen, about 280 words at most: the number, its n, the Source line.
- **A report gets the read first, then the tables.** Offer a Doc or Sheet for the whole book. Never force one, and never paste raw JSON.

## The one-screen read (every report)

1. **One line: whose film and what span.** "Their offense, 2026, 8 games, 600 competitive snaps." (Every number in this file is made up for illustration.)
2. **Three to five findings that change a call.** Each is one line, the look and the rate with its n and tier, in coach words:
   - "From 2x2 they throw 70% (105/150, HIGH) against 50% overall."
   - "Counter is their most explosive run: 20% go 12+ (10/50, HIGH)."
3. **What the data can't say**, once, when it matters to the ask.
4. **The Source line**, word for word.

## Tables, when he wants the book

- **Keep the report's own section order and labels.** Rows read `label | count | rate (count/n)`.
- **Keep the LOW tags and the dashes.** Don't round a rate the report printed.
- **Each table carries its Source line.** Note the definitions once, under the first table that uses them.
- **Sheet:** one tab per section, plus the definitions and the games list.
- **Doc:** the read on page 1, then one heading per section.

## Words

- **Use the coach's football words, not ours.** Write "explosive run", never "shot play". Write "pressured", never "pressure rate" (pressure rate is ambiguous). Write the band labels as printed.
- **Never put on a coach's screen:** denominator, universe, pool, gate, ledger, verify, provenance, join, field names, raw codes, file names, tool names.
- **Voice:**
  - An opponent scout says "they" and "their".
  - A self-scout says "you" and "your" about his unit, and "they" about the teams he faced.
  - Report, don't sell, and don't prescribe.
- **Source and football knowledge:**
  - Call it "play-by-play data". The Source line the tool prints is the only source line.
  - Never cite a podcast, a coach, a book or a knowledge base for football knowledge.
  - General football that does not come from his data is labelled "general, not from your data".
