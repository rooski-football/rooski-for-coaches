# What's new

## 0.4.0 — November 2026
- Calls Rooski's permanent tool names (D2, 2026-11-20). Your AI does the same things as before; if your Rooski plan still uses the older names, it uses those.

## 0.3.1 — November 2026
- Rooski Drive: the skill's short summary is trimmed so every Claude app reads all of it, including the part that tells your AI when not to use it. Nothing else changed.

## 0.3.0 — November 2026
- Rooski Drive: on a paid plan, you can now share your `! Rooski Drive` folder with Rooski, and on Sundays Rooski moves leftover temp files from Word, Excel and PowerPoint, and exact copies, into your old-versions folder. Every move is written down before it happens, and your AI puts that list in your Drive, tells you what moved, and can put any file back. Rooski never deletes, never makes or renames files in your Drive, and never changes what's in them. To stop it, un-share the folder. Your AI asks at most once a season.

## 0.2.1 — November 2026
- Rooski Drive: your AI now takes your plan and your Drive's place straight from the Rooski connector, copies each week's files from the right master (a head coach's practice plan comes from his head coach folder), and the weekly tidy points out files left loose and how many are waiting in your Inbox. Nothing moves without your yes, and nothing is ever deleted.

## 0.2.0 — November 2026
- New: the **Rooski Drive** skill. Say "set up my Rooski Drive" and your AI builds your football folders in your own Google Drive, then keeps them tidy: a quick check each chat, a weekly clean-up, opening each game week (playoffs too), moving into your program's drive, and putting the season away when it ends. Nothing moves without your yes, nothing is ever deleted, and every move is written down so it can be undone. Needs the Rooski connector and Google Drive.

## 0.1.2 — November 2026
- First release: the **scout reports** skill. Ask for an opponent scout, a self-scout or a tendency study and your AI builds it from your program's data, with every number's sample size and source.
