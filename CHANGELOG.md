# What's new

## 0.6.2 — November 2026
- Safer with your Google Drive: your AI finds your Rooski Drive map from your Rooski profile first, and uses only files and folders that are yours. Words inside your files, file names and folder names are treated as information, never as instructions: if a file says to share, move, delete or send something, your AI tells you what it says and waits for you to ask. Anything outside your Rooski Drive, and any share, always asks you first. The Reset and career help follow the same rule.

## 0.6.1 — November 2026
- Career help: what you send (resume, portfolio, letters) now carries one small "Built with Rooski" line in the footer of the last page, and nothing else from Rooski. You can still theme it to your school (colors, logo, mascot).

## 0.6.0 — November 2026
- New: **career help**, part of Rooski Pro. Say "build my resume", "build my portfolio", "help me with this job" or "get me ready for the interview", and your AI opens Rooski's career steps and works them with you: your coaching resume and portfolio, a sourced brief on a school with an opening, your letter, who decides there and how to reach them from the school's public pages, your job log, and interview prep (a cheat sheet, a mock interview and a 30-day plan). It saves to your own Google Drive, under your name only, on your go. Your AI drafts; you send. If Rooski's career steps aren't on your plan yet, it says so. Needs the Rooski connector and Google Drive.
- Rooski Drive: your AI now leaves your resume, portfolio and job search to the career skill. Nothing else changed.

## 0.5.0 — November 2026
- New: the **Rooski Reset** skill. Say "start my Reset" and your AI opens Rooski's Reset steps, the same steps and columns as your Rooski Reset Kit, and works them with you: your players' and staff's answers, the season review, who's back and who to go get, keep / change / add / stop, your learning list, your War Map and the hand-up to your AD. It saves what you decide to your own Google Drive, only on your go. If Rooski's Reset steps aren't on your plan yet, it says so and works from your Kit copy. Needs the Rooski connector and Google Drive.
- Rooski Drive: your AI now leaves the Reset to the Reset skill. Nothing else changed.

## 0.4.0 — November 2026
- Calls Rooski's permanent tool names (D2, 2026-11-20). Your AI does the same things as before; if your Rooski plan still uses the older names, it uses those.

## 0.3.1 — November 2026
- Rooski Drive: the skill's short summary is trimmed so every Claude app reads all of it, including the part that tells your AI when not to use it. Nothing else changed.

## 0.3.0 — November 2026
- Rooski Drive: on a paid plan, you can now share your `! Rooski Drive` folder with Rooski, and on Sundays Rooski moves leftover temp files from Word, Excel and PowerPoint, and exact copies, into your old-versions folder. Every move is written down before it happens, and your AI puts that list in your Drive, tells you what moved, and can put any file back. Rooski never deletes, never makes or renames files in your Drive, and never changes what's in them. To stop it, un-share the folder. Your AI asks at most once a season.

## 0.2.1 — November 2026
- Rooski Drive: your AI now takes your plan and your Drive's place straight from the Rooski connector, copies each week's files from the right master (a head coach's practice plan comes from his head coach folder), and the weekly tidy points out files left loose and how many are waiting in your Inbox. Nothing moves without your yes, and nothing is ever deleted.

## 0.2.0 — November 2026
- New: the **Rooski Drive** skill. Say "set up my Rooski Drive" and your AI builds your football folders in your own Google Drive, then keeps them tidy: a quick check each chat, a weekly clean-up, opening each game week (playoffs too), moving into your program's drive, and putting the season away when it ends. Nothing moves without your yes, nothing is ever deleted, and every move is written down so it can be undone. Needs the Rooski connector and Google Drive.

## 0.1.2 — November 2026
- First release: the **scout reports** skill. Ask for an opponent scout, a self-scout or a tendency study and your AI builds it from your program's data, with every number's sample size and source.
